## 2. What data we process

- **Account**: your name, email address, password (stored only as a one-way hash), second-factor secret and recovery codes (when you enable TOTP), the avatar you pick or upload, and your saved preferences.
- **Organizations and exercises**: the organizations you belong to, your roles in them and in each exercise, and what you write in the service (notes, answers, ratings, feedback).
- **Sessions and security**: when you signed in, the IP address and browser of each session, and sign-in events (successful and failed) in the security log.
- **Legal acceptances**: which version of these documents you accepted and when.
- **Activity log**: your sign-ins (successful and failed), sign-outs and changes to how you sign in (password, two-factor authentication), and what this service's administrators do to your account (suspending or reactivating it, making it an administrator) or to an organization: who did it, to whom and when, with the email of each at the time. It may be forwarded to the operator's security monitoring system.

## 3. Why, and on what legal basis

- To provide the service you signed up for: your account, your organizations and their exercises (performance of a contract).
- To keep the service and your account secure: sessions, two-factor authentication, security logs, preventing abuse (our legitimate interest).
- To keep proof that you accepted these documents, and to meet legal obligations that apply to us (legal obligation and legitimate interest).

We don't sell your data, don't use it for advertising and don't profile you.

## 6. How long we keep it

- Account data, while your account exists. When you delete it, it's deactivated and anonymized: your name and email are removed, and what you wrote inside an organization's exercises stays with that organization without your identity.
- Session records, while your account exists; security and access logs rotate and are overwritten as they grow.

## 7. Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, its correction or erasure, to restrict or object to its processing, and to receive it in a portable format, by writing to {{contact_email}}. You can delete your account yourself from Preferences. You may also complain to your data protection supervisory authority.

## 8. Security

Passwords are hashed with Argon2, sessions use HttpOnly cookies protected against cross-site request forgery and expire after inactivity, and roles that handle sensitive exercise content must use two-factor authentication.

## 9. Cookies and local storage

The service uses only what it needs to work: a session cookie, a cookie that protects against cross-site request forgery, and your browser's local storage for preferences such as the theme or a folded menu. There are no advertising or analytics cookies.

When this policy changes substantially, you'll be asked to accept the new version before you go on using the service.
