# Issue an approved holding statement consistent with the facts

## Objective

Within 90 minutes of media or customer attention, communications publishes an approved holding statement consistent with the facts the incident team has confirmed, and staff receive an internal message through a channel that still works. For listed companies, the statement is aligned with what the disclosure committee has decided.

## Success criterion

A holding statement is drafted from a prepared template or from scratch; legal and the incident manager approve it before release; it contains no fact the incident team has not confirmed; staff receive an internal message through a channel independent of the affected systems; one spokesperson is named.

## Evidence

The approved statement and its approval trail, the internal message and the channel used, and the media log kept by communications. A mock press call inject produces useful evidence of how the spokesperson handles questions.

## Regulatory mapping

Evidences crisis communication plans under DORA (DORA-11). For US-listed companies, it tests the consistency between public statements and the materiality determination and disclosure under Form 8-K Item 1.05 (SEC-8K-105).

## Common pitfalls

Saying too much too early (a number of affected customers that later doubles), or saying nothing while social media fills the gap; different messages from different people; and staff learning from the press. Prepared templates for the first hour are a frequent improvement action.
