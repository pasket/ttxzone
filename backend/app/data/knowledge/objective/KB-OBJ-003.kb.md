# Convene the crisis committee and agree first priorities

## Objective

Within 45 minutes of the severity reaching the crisis threshold, the crisis committee meets with its chair or a designated deputy and agrees its first priorities, which are written down and shared with the response teams. The committee's composition comes from the crisis plan; the exercise checks that it can actually assemble and decide.

## Success criterion

The committee is convened by the person the crisis plan names; the chair or a designated deputy leads the meeting; at least three priorities are agreed and written down; the priorities are communicated to the response teams; the next meeting time is set.

## Evidence

The convocation message, the minutes or decision log with the priorities, and the message passing them to the teams. In a tabletop, a facilitator can ask for the priorities on a flip chart and photograph it.

## Regulatory mapping

Evidences crisis management under NIS2 (NIS2-21-2-C) and the business continuity procedures of ISO 22301 clause 8.5 (incident response structure, communication and decisions). It is also the moment the executive accountability expected by most frameworks becomes visible.

## Common pitfalls

The committee meets but discusses technical details instead of priorities; the chair is absent and nobody knows who deputizes; decisions are taken but not communicated. Name a deputy for every role in the exercise plan and include an inject that removes the chair.
