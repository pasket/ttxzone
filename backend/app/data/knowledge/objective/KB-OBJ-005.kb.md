# Assess a personal data breach and decide on notification

## Objective

Once personal data is known or suspected to be affected, the data protection officer leads an assessment of the risk to the people concerned and the team decides, with reasons, whether to notify the supervisory authority within 72 hours of awareness and whether to inform the people affected.

## Success criterion

The data protection officer is involved; the categories and approximate number of people and records are stated; the risk to individuals is assessed and recorded; a decision on notifying the authority is recorded with its reasons; a decision on informing the people affected is recorded; the breach register entry is opened.

## Evidence

The breach assessment, the breach register entry, the draft notification and the draft message to individuals if the risk is high. Note in the decision log when the team considered itself aware, because the 72 hours run from there.

## Regulatory mapping

Evidences GDPR Articles 33 and 34 (GDPR-33-34) and, through the exercise itself, the regular testing of organizational measures under Article 32(1)(d) (GDPR-32-1-D). The same structure works for the UK GDPR.

## Common pitfalls

Treating a suspected exfiltration as no breach until proven; not knowing which systems hold personal data, so numbers cannot be estimated; and leaving the data protection officer out of the first hours. A data inventory linked to the incident procedure is a common improvement action.
