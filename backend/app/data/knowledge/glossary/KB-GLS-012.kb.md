# Simulated actors and the simulation cell

## Definition

Simulated actors are everyone who sends injects or answers the players without being one of them: the regulator, the bank, a cloud provider, a journalist, an angry customer, the threat actor's ransom note. The simulation cell is the part of the control team that plays them, answering calls and emails in character so the players' actions meet a realistic response.

## In an exercise

Each inject names its sender among the simulated actors, with the delivery method that actor would use. Before the session, assign who plays each actor and give them short notes on what they know and how they behave, so the answers stay consistent when players improvise.

## Common confusions

Simulated actors are not Observers, who watch without interacting, nor Players from another organization taking part. A team that is not playing (for example the security operations centre in an executive exercise) becomes a simulated actor for that session.
