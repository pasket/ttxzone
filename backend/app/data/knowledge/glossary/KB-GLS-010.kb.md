# Time of awareness

## Definition

Notification rules usually count their deadlines from when the organization becomes aware: of a significant incident under the EU network and information security directive, of a personal data breach under data protection law, of a disruption under critical-entity rules. Awareness generally means having a reasonable degree of certainty that an incident occurred, not full knowledge of its cause or scope.

## In an exercise

In an exercise, ask the table to state when it considers itself aware and record that scenario time, then measure each report against its deadline from there. Injects that bring suspicion first and confirmation later are a good way to discuss when awareness starts.

## Common confusions

Awareness is not the moment the first alert fired, nor the moment the investigation is finished. Waiting for certainty before starting the clock is a frequent and risky misreading, since early reports are meant to be incomplete.
