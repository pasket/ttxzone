# After-action report and improvement plan

## Definition

The after-action report (AAR) summarizes the exercise (scope, scenario, participants), rates each objective, and lists strengths and areas for improvement with the observations behind them and a recommendation for each gap. The improvement plan turns the recommendations into corrective actions, each with an owner, a deadline and a way to check it is done, and is tracked until closure.

## In an exercise

The report is drafted from the evaluation guides, the hot wash and the feedback forms, reviewed with the planning team and approved by the sponsor. Improvement actions can be linked to a later exercise that re-tests them, closing the loop between exercises and making progress visible over a programme.

## Common confusions

The report is not a transcript of the session nor a list of who made mistakes. An improvement plan without owners and dates is a wish list; regulators and auditors usually ask for evidence that actions were closed, not just written.
