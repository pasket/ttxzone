# Situation manual

## Definition

A situation manual (SitMan) is given to participants at the start of a tabletop exercise. It explains why the exercise is held, what is in and out of scope, the objectives, the assumptions and artificialities, how play works, and the scenario as it stands at the start of each phase, often with the key questions to discuss. It never contains the list of injects.

## In an exercise

Planners prepare it from the needs analysis and the MSEL once the design is ready, and print or share it just before the session. During play it lets participants refer back to the situation and the questions while the Controller delivers new information.

## Common confusions

The situation manual is not the Controller and Facilitator guide, which holds the injects, expected actions and notes for the control team. It is also distinct from the invitation or agenda sent beforehand.
