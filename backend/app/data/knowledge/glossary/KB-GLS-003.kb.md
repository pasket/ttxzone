# Inject: mandatory and contingent

## Definition

An inject is one message delivered to the players during an exercise to move the scenario forward or to prompt a decision: a monitoring alert, a supplier's email, a regulator's call, a journalist's question, a social media post. A mandatory inject is always delivered at its time and carries the storyline. A contingent inject is delivered only if a stated condition occurs, usually when the players miss an expected action, to steer them back or to show a consequence.

## In an exercise

Each inject names its sender (a simulated actor voiced by the control team), its recipients among the participating areas, the delivery method, and the expected actions with the time in which they should happen. It may carry images or files, such as a log extract or a screenshot, and can be rendered to look like the real thing (an email or a press release), always marked as simulated.

## Common confusions

An inject is not a question to the table; key questions belong to the phase and are put by the Facilitator. A contingent inject is not a spare inject used to fill time: it exists because a specific action might not happen.
