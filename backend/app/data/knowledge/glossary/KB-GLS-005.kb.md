# P/S/M/U rating scale

## Definition

The four levels are: P, performed without challenges (the objective was met and no gaps were seen); S, performed with some challenges (met, with opportunities to improve); M, performed with major challenges (met only partly, or with gaps that could harm the outcome); U, unable to be performed (not met). Each level is defined by what was observed, so two Evaluators reach the same rating from the same facts.

## In an exercise

Evaluators rate each observation point and then the objective as a whole, with a short justification. Planners may adjust how many conditions must be met for each level, for instance requiring every condition for P. The ratings appear in the after-action report alongside the observations that support them.

## Common confusions

The scale is not a score of people, and a U is not a failure of the exercise: finding what cannot be done is the purpose. Writing a rating level into a success criterion (for example "performed with some challenges") confuses the criterion with its evaluation.
