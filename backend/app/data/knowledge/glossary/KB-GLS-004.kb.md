# Exercise evaluation guide (EEG)

## Definition

An exercise evaluation guide (EEG) translates each objective into what an Evaluator should look for and how to rate it. It lists the objective, its success criterion broken into observable conditions (the observation points), any time boxes, and space for notes and a rating per condition and per objective. Using the same guide for every Evaluator makes ratings comparable.

## In an exercise

Evaluators fill the guide in during the session, timing actions on the scenario clock and noting who did what. Afterwards their ratings and notes feed the after-action report, where each objective is rated and each gap becomes a finding with a recommendation and, in the improvement plan, an owner and a deadline.

## Common confusions

The guide does not evaluate individuals: it rates how the organization's plans and capabilities performed. It is not the participant feedback form, which asks players about the exercise itself, nor the hot wash, which collects their impressions.
