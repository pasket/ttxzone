# Master scenario events list (MSEL)

## Definition

The master scenario events list (MSEL) is the backbone of an exercise's conduct. It lists every inject in order with when it is delivered (or what triggers it), who sends it, who receives it, its content and the expected actions that show whether the objectives are met. It is built from the approved objectives so that every inject serves at least one of them.

## In an exercise

Controllers run the session from the MSEL, releasing mandatory injects at their time and firing contingent ones when the table does or fails to do something. Evaluators use its expected actions as observation points. Because it reveals the whole storyline, it is shared only with the planning and control team, never with the players before the session.

## Common confusions

The MSEL is not the scenario narrative (that is the background the players receive) and not the situation manual handed to the players. It is also distinct from a run-of-show agenda: it contains the content, not just the timing of breaks and briefings.
