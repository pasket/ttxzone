# STARTEX and ENDEX

## Definition

STARTEX marks the moment play begins and ENDEX the moment it ends. Declaring both out loud, and in writing, separates exercise communications from real ones and tells participants when the simulated world starts and stops. A closing message at ENDEX tells every room that play is over.

## In an exercise

The Controller declares STARTEX after the briefing and ENDEX when the last objective has been covered or time runs out, before the hot wash. A stop-exercise procedure with an agreed keyword allows anyone to halt play at any time if a real incident or a safety issue occurs.

## Common confusions

ENDEX is not the end of the session: the hot wash and administrative close follow it. A pause is not ENDEX either: play can resume after a pause, whereas after ENDEX nothing more is delivered.
