# Network code on cybersecurity for electricity: crisis management and exercises

## Scope

A sector-specific regulation for electricity: transmission and distribution system operators, large generators, market operators and other entities whose compromise could affect cross-border flows, classified by impact. It complements NIS2 and CER for the same organizations rather than replacing them. TTX-Zone offers it only where the needs analysis says the exercise concerns the electricity sector.

## What it expects from exercises

The code builds a cycle of cybersecurity risk assessments at Union, regional and entity level, with minimum and advanced controls for high-impact and critical-impact entities. It asks entities to have cybersecurity crisis management and incident response capabilities and to take part in cybersecurity exercises organized at national or regional level, which makes a tabletop with grid-specific injects (loss of visibility in the control room, manipulated setpoints, a neighbouring operator in trouble) directly relevant.

## Notification duties

The code relies on the incident reporting channels of NIS2 and adds information sharing on cyber attacks with cross-border relevance between operators, their CSIRTs and the regional coordination centres. In an exercise, the useful question is who informs neighbouring operators and how fast, in addition to the NIS2 reports.

## Evidence to keep

The entity's risk assessment and the controls chosen for its impact class, the crisis management plan, records of participation in exercises with their findings, and information-sharing arrangements. Keep the actions from each exercise linked to the next risk assessment cycle.

## Exercise hooks

Use operational technology injects with a physical consequence and a cross-border dimension: a neighbouring operator reports anomalies, the control room loses telemetry, a market actor asks questions. Test the hand-off between the IT security team and the control room. Map objectives to NCCS-CRISIS, together with the NIS2 reporting requirements.
