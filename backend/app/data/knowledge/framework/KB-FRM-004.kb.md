# DORA: ICT continuity, major incident reporting and resilience testing in finance

## Scope

Banks, payment and e-money institutions, investment firms, insurers, crypto-asset service providers, trading venues and other financial entities, with proportionality for small ones. Critical ICT third-party providers fall under an oversight framework of their own. Being a regulation, it applies directly without national transposition, supplemented by technical standards that fix classification criteria, templates and deadlines.

## What it expects from exercises

Article 11 asks for an ICT business continuity policy and response and recovery plans, tested regularly (at least yearly, and after substantive changes), including scenarios of cyber attacks and switchovers, with crisis communication plans. Articles 24 to 26 set up a digital operational resilience testing programme proportionate to the entity, with advanced threat-led penetration testing for the most significant ones. Management bodies are accountable and must keep their knowledge current, which makes executive tabletops a natural fit.

## Notification duties

Article 19: major ICT-related incidents are reported to the competent authority with an initial notification, an intermediate report and a final report, on harmonized templates. Under the technical standards the initial notification is due within hours of classifying the incident as major (and no later than a day after becoming aware), the intermediate report within days, and the final report within a month; check the current standard for the exact figures. Clients may need to be informed when their financial interests are affected.

## Evidence to keep

Test reports of continuity plans, the classification of each incident against the criteria, the reports sent, the register of ICT third-party arrangements and the lessons learned. Supervisors expect findings from tests to feed a remediation plan reported to the management body.

## Exercise hooks

Have the table classify the incident against the major-incident criteria (clients affected, duration, geographic spread, data losses, criticality of services, economic impact) and race the initial notification. Make a critical ICT provider the source of the outage to test exit and substitution options. Map objectives to DORA-11, DORA-19 and DORA-24.
