# Creator → Session plan (evaluation plan and conduct preparation)

The last design step, after the approved needs analysis (what is validated) and the READY MSEL (how it is provoked): **how the exercise is measured** and **how its session is secured**. Section `conduct-prep` (`/creator/:ttxId/conduct-prep`), fourth in `CREATOR_SECTIONS` (right below MSEL; menu and page title "Session plan" / "Plan de la sesión"; internally still `conduct_prep` / `conduct-prep`), behind `CreatorDesignGate`. Planner-only (design): every route is in `DESIGN_ENDPOINTS`.

## Model (`models/ttx_conduct_prep.py`, migration `14cadc58f759`)
- `TTXConductPrep` — one per TTX, created on the first `GET` with an approved needs form (409 `NEEDS_FORM_NOT_APPROVED` otherwise; the form row is locked while creating). Same conventions as the MSEL: text defaults to `""`, `revision` bumps on every save (optimistic lock, 409 `CONDUCT_PREP_STALE`), `version` bumps when a READY plan is edited (`_reopen`), `ready_by/_id/_at` stamped by the server. `seeded_needs_form_version` / `seeded_msel_version` are the sources the EEGs were last built from.
  - Evaluation: `data_collection_methods[]`, `data_collection_other`, `log_keeper_id` (a planning and control team member), `briefing_date`, `briefing_checklist[]`, `briefing_notes`, `briefed_controller_ids[]` (the controller and evaluator briefing — shown in the session step; Controllers' attendance here since they have no evaluator row, migration `a7c3e9d2f164`), the evaluation calendar (shown in Documents and follow-up) as **days after the session** (`hot_wash_days`, `debrief_days`, `ratings_due_days`, `draft_aar_days`, `after_action_meeting_days`; 0–365, defaults `CALENDAR_DEFAULTS` 0/0/3/14/21 — a date can rarely be fixed before the exercise). Migration `d8a3f6b2c914` turned earlier dates into days (and snapshot payloads too); the UI explains each milestone and shows its date once the session has one.
  - Design validation: `fpm_date`, `fpm_done`, `fpm_attendees`, `fpm_notes`, `fpm_msel_version` (stamped with the latest READY MSEL version when `fpm_done` turns on), `dry_run_date`, `dry_run_done`.
  - Logistics: `session_date` + `session_time` + `timezone` (IANA, validated with `zoneinfo`) — a wall-clock date and time in a zone, correct across DST; reads derive `session_starts_at` (UTC). `modality` (in_person / remote / hybrid), `venue`, `room`, `meeting_link` (`https://` only), `session_notes` (optional complementary information, any modality), `logistics_checklist[]`; Convocation: `invitation_sent_on`, `invitation_text` (the invitation as sent, optional, ≤ 20 000 chars; migration `c3f8a2d6e915`) (the old `absence_risk_id` was dropped by `b9e4f1a7c2d5`).
  - Documents: `documents_reviewed[]`, `stop_procedure` (seeded in the creator's language).
  - Vocabularies (`DATA_COLLECTION_METHODS`, `BRIEFING_ITEMS`, `LOGISTICS_ITEMS`, `DOCUMENT_KINDS`) live in `schemas/ttx_conduct_prep.py`, mirrored in `lib/conductPrep.ts`.
- `ConductPrepEegObjective` — the definitive EEG of one needs objective (`objective_id` without FK; code, statement and capability copied). `ConductPrepEegPoint` — `kind` (criterion / expected_action / notification / custom), `text`, `criteria_p/s/m/u` (optional adjustments of the **common rating scale**: blank = the common definition of that level, `creator.needs.outputs.ratingDefinitions` / PDF `level_definitions`), and the `source_*` fields it was seeded from (`source_action_id` without FK: an MSEL save deletes actions). A seeded point's `text` is always its `source_text`: the clause or the expected action is edited at its source (needs analysis / MSEL), never here (`save_prep` and the dry run ignore the payload's text for non-custom points; migration `b2e7c4a19d03` reset points rewritten before). Only `custom` points take their text from the plan, and only they can be removed here: a seeded point left out of a save is kept (after the listed ones) — it goes when its clause or expected action goes at the source. No "observable and measurable" tick (column dropped by `c5d1e8f30a47`): the point's text already says what is observed and against what.
- `ConductPrepEvaluator` (+ `ConductPrepEvaluatorObjective`) — an exercise Evaluator as the plan assigns them: `areas[]` (needs `participating_areas`), `briefed`, the EEGs they cover (the free-text expertise was dropped by `f2a9d6c3b7e1`). UI: "Evaluator assignments" is a coverage matrix — objective rows (and, optional, participating-area rows) × the exercise's Evaluators, a checkbox per cell, a Covered / "No evaluator" column; the people and the Lead Evaluator come from the exercise team (linked, never edited here); "Attended the briefing" lives in the evaluator briefing group.
- `ConductPrepFinding` (design findings: `found_in` — `design_review` (the sponsor's or a stakeholder's review of the drafts) / `planning_meeting` / `dry_run`, migration `c7a4e2f9b816`, older rows and snapshots read as the dry run's — kind, description, inject or phase by id without FK, `resolved`; their own optional "Design findings" group, fourth in the design validation step, a new row taking the previous row's review), `ConductPrepAttendee` (key decision makers: `user_id` — a Player of the exercise, no FK; their name is the account's, refreshed on read and stored on save — or null for someone added by hand; name, role, `confirmed`, `fallback` substitute / postpone / proceed, `substitute`; a `user_id` that isn't a Player, or repeats, is saved as a plain name). The context carries `exercise_players` (approved Players with `invited_at` = when they used the invitation, the latest approved `TTXAccessRequest`, and `joined_at` = their `TTXAccess`) and `pending_access_requests`. UI (Session → Convocation and key decision makers, optional): a "Players with access" list (name, email, "Used the invitation on …", Add / ✓ Decision maker, the pending count linking to Players → Pending, an empty state linking to Players → Invitations), then the decision makers as rows — a Player's name read-only with "Player · access since …", a manual row's name input with "No access to TTX-Zone" — role, an attendance toggle (`aria-pressed`), and, while not confirmed, the absence choice as inline radios (the stand-in input only for "Someone stands in"), `ConductPrepSnapshot` (JSONB, written when READY).
- Deletion: `delete_conduct_preps_for_ttx` runs in `delete_organization` before `delete_msels_for_ttx`. An Evaluator leaving the exercise (any path through `_delete_access_with_tags`, or `set_role` away from Evaluator) loses their assignment (`drop_evaluator`).
- The Lead Evaluator is not on the plan: it's `TTXAccess.leads` (see `creator-exercise.md`), read into `context.lead_evaluator`.

## Seeding and drift (`services/ttx_conduct_prep_service.py`)
- On creation each objective gets an EEG: one `criterion` point per condition (line) of its success criterion (`eeg_service.observation_points`) and one `expected_action` point per expected action of the **latest READY MSEL snapshot** that evidences that objective (the action's `objective_ids`, see `msel.md`) (never the live MSEL: its version only moves when it leaves READY). The session time starts as the MSEL's `exercise_start`.
- `drift.needs_changed` (approved needs form version ≠ seeded) / `drift.msel_changed` (latest READY snapshot version ≠ seeded) block readiness (E07). `POST …/eeg/reseed` merges: expected-action points take their action's new text (ratings kept), new clauses/actions are appended, seeded points whose source is gone are dropped (a reworded clause is a new point; the old one and its ratings go), EEGs of objectives gone from the needs analysis are dropped with their assignments, new objectives get one. It reopens a READY plan.
- Notification points (migration `f6c2a9d4b813`: enum value + `source_requirement_id`): one per notification requirement the objective cites (`notification_reference.notification_point_text`, in the language of the request that seeds it — open or reseed, both read `Accept-Language`); orphaned once the objective stops citing it, dropped by the reseed, added by it when newly cited.
- Per point `source_status`: custom, current, changed (its MSEL action changed since; cleared by the reseed), orphaned (until the reseed drops it; an action that stops evidencing the objective orphans its point there even while other EEGs list it).

## Save, rules, lifecycle
- `PUT` is a whole-document save: EEGs are neither created nor removed by it (only their points, full replace by id); evaluators, findings and attendees full-replace by id (422 `CONDUCT_PREP_ROW_NOT_FOUND`); evaluators must be the exercise's Evaluators, once each (422 `NOT_AN_EVALUATOR`); links to unknown EEGs are dropped. Read-only (409) while the needs analysis isn't APPROVED.
- `validation_report` (pure; `draft_report` is the dry run of `POST …/validate`): `ready` blocks DRAFT → READY, `warnings` never block, `approved` is unused. **READY needs only a small core** — every objective measured (its EEG, seeded; an Evaluator on it), the design fixed (MSEL READY, mandatory injects validated), the session set (when, where, how) and how it stops (seeded). Everything else is optional, scaled to the exercise: checked for consistency once filled in (a date after the session, a recorded finding unresolved, a row without a name), and only a few gaps worth a look warn. Checklists (briefing, room and materials, documents reviewed), attendance ticks, the invitation date and the dry run never raise an issue. Sections = the page's steps: 1 evaluation plan, 2 design validation, 3 session (session, stop procedure, convocation and decision makers, room and materials, briefing), 4 documents and follow-up (`CALENDAR_ORDER`), 5 review (no issues of its own).
  - E01–E08: an EEG per objective (`EEG_OBJECTIVE_MISSING`, `EEG_OBJECTIVE_ORPHANED`), points present and written (P/S/M/U are never required: the common scale applies); `EEG_SOURCE_CHANGED` (drift); warnings `EEG_POINT_VAGUE` (custom points only — seeded wording is checked at its source; the needs form's vague-word list), `EEG_POINT_SOURCE_CHANGED`.
  - E09–E17: `OBJECTIVE_WITHOUT_EVALUATOR`, warnings `AREA_WITHOUT_EVALUATOR` (once areas are used) and `LEAD_EVALUATOR_MISSING`; `DATA_COLLECTION_REQUIRED` only for "other" without its text (a new plan starts with `DEFAULT_DATA_COLLECTION`: observation notes, hot wash, feedback forms), `LOG_KEEPER_NOT_ON_TEAM` once one is set; warning `BRIEFING_DATE_REQUIRED`, `BRIEFING_AFTER_SESSION`; warning `CALENDAR_ORDER` (the calendar can't be missing or before the session: it's days after it with defaults).
  - E30–E36: `MSEL_NOT_READY`, `INJECTS_NOT_VALIDATED` (mandatory injects of the live MSEL), warning `FPM_NOT_DONE`, `FPM_AFTER_SESSION`, warning `DESIGN_CHANGED_AFTER_FPM`, `DRY_RUN_AFTER_SESSION`, `FINDING_INCOMPLETE`/`FINDING_UNRESOLVED`.
  - E40–E47: `SESSION_INCOMPLETE` (date, time, zone, modality; venue in person/hybrid; link remote/hybrid), `SESSION_OUTSIDE_WINDOW` (needs form date window), warning `SESSION_START_MISMATCH` (≠ MSEL start); warning `DECISION_MAKERS_REQUIRED` (none listed), `ATTENDEE_INCOMPLETE`, `DECISION_MAKER_WITHOUT_FALLBACK` (only a chosen stand-in without a name; the absence choice itself is optional), warning `SESSION_POSTPONE_PENDING`.
  - E20–E21: `STOP_PROCEDURE_REQUIRED`, warning `STOP_KEYWORD_MISSING`.
- `POST …/transitions` (`draft`/`ready`, no step-up — a sign-off undone by going back to draft): READY needs an empty `ready` bucket (409 `CONDUCT_PREP_INVALID`), stamps `ready_*` and writes a snapshot.

## EEG for Control and Execution
`GET /ttx/{id}/eeg` (`eeg_service.build_eeg`) serves the latest `ConductPrepSnapshot` when there is one (`source: "plan"`, `plan_version`, `plan_reopened` while a newer draft exists, `lead_evaluator`; each objective's `points` with `criteria` keyed P/S/M/U and its `evaluators`), else the derived draft (`source: "derived"`). `EegView` shows the common scale (level → definition) once and, per point, only the levels it adjusts; `ExecutionEvaluationSection` says when it's a draft or when a new version is being prepared.

## Session documents (`services/conduct_prep_pdf_service.py`, copy `conduct_prep_pdf_labels.py` merged over `msel_pdf_labels.py`)
`GET …/conduct-prep/export/{kind}.pdf?lang=`: `sitman`, `facilitator-guide`, `inject-cards`, `eeg`, `evaluation-plan`, `feedback-form`. The MSEL comes from `msel_pdf_service._source` (READY snapshot, or a live MSEL without errors — 409 `MSEL_INCOMPLETE`); the plan from its READY snapshot, else live with a "Not final" notice. EXERCISE band and TLP on every page; header `Plan vN · MSEL vM`; `{code}_{kind}_v{plan}_{TLP}.pdf`, `attachment` + `no-store`.
- SitMan: `SitmanExport` gains the session (date, start, zone, modality, venue, link) and the Facilitators' names, plus non-attribution rules, and first the stop procedure with the keyword (the needs analysis' or «REAL WORLD», what the Playground's button asks for) — still no injects, expected actions, notes or triggers.
- Facilitator and Controller guide: the plan's stop procedure and the keyword, the session, the Lead Evaluator, the log keeper, the evaluator roster and the calendar ("N days after (date)", `_deadline`), then the MSEL by phase; last, the expected-notifications sheet when an objective cites a notification requirement.
- EEG: the common scale once (level → definition), then per objective who evaluates it, its time box (from the needs objective), each point with a table of only the levels it adjusts, the evidence as a `[ ]` checklist, the rating as a grid of the scale's boxes beside what each outcome means here (`_rating_grid`: `[ ] P [ ] S` achieved — the rule and its note; `[ ] M` partially; `[ ] U` not achieved; a blank level prints the common definition) and notes; `EegView` shows the same (`common/RatingLevels`, also in the objective dialog's evaluator view); then the expected-notifications sheet (`_notifications_story`: per regime a row per report — report and recipient, deadline, clock start — with I/D/C/O marks to tick and room for the simulated time). `GET /ttx/{id}/eeg` carries the same as `notifications` (from the needs objectives, plan or derived).
- Evaluation plan: session, roster, data collection, briefing, calendar, rules for evaluators, rating scale.
- Feedback form (`FeedbackFormExport`: objectives and date only): conduct and logistics statements and one per objective rated 1–5, then strengths, areas for improvement, plan changes, comments.
