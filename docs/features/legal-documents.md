# Legal documents (terms of service, privacy policy)

Each deployment's operator — not the project — writes and publishes the terms of service and the privacy policy its users accept: the software licence (AGPL-3.0-or-later, `LICENSE`/`NOTICE`) covers the code, these documents cover the use of one running instance, whose operator is the data controller. **While nothing is published, nothing is asked** (signup shows no checkbox, no account is held), so an internal instance can skip them.

## Operator: Platform → Legal documents
`components/platform/PlatformLegalPanel.tsx`, instance administrator only (TOTP mandatory).

- **Start from template** asks once for the operator's name, contact email, postal address (optional) and applicable law/courts, renders the shipped templates (`backend/app/data/legal/{terms,privacy}.{en,es}.md`, `{{operator_name}}`/`{{contact_email}}`/`{{postal_address}}`/`{{jurisdiction}}` placeholders) and writes them into both drafts — replacing what the drafts held, never a published version. From then on the text is the operator's own: there are no live variables, so changing a detail means editing and publishing. The templates are generic and say so in their first paragraph (review with a legal adviser, delete that paragraph before publishing; `[Adapt: …]` marks what depends on the deployment: providers, hosting, transfers).
- **One panel per document**, a tab per supported language, the `FormattedTextArea` Markdown subset plus `# `/`## `/`### ` titles (`FormattedText`'s `headings`). A language left empty isn't offered. **Save draft** (`LegalDocumentDraft`, one row per document); **Publish…** (only with a saved draft that differs from the latest version) asks whether the change is **substantial** (every account accepts it again) or a **minor correction** (published and recorded, no one asked). The first version is always substantial. Publishing needs a recent authentication (step-up).
- **Published versions** (`LegalDocumentVersion`, immutable: the exact text users accepted): number, date, author, kind of change; each opens read-only in every language it has.
- **Download acceptance record (CSV)**, once something is published: every acceptance, oldest first — UTC time, email, name, document, version — for an audit (`GET /instance/legal/acceptances.csv`, step-up: it names every account; a deleted account appears anonymized; formula-like cells kept as text).

## Users
- **Signup**, in all three register modes (plain account, *Set up my organization*, token redemption creating an account): one checkbox for what's published ("I accept the terms of service and the privacy policy", or one of them), a link per document opening `common/LegalDocumentDialog`. The body carries `accepted_legal: [{kind, version}]`; the backend checks it **before** creating anything (400 `LEGAL_ACCEPTANCE_REQUIRED` when a published document is missing, 409 `LEGAL_VERSION_OUTDATED` when a newer version came out while the form was open — the form reloads the documents and asks again) and records it right after. Should recording ever fail, the account just meets the acceptance screen on its next request — never an account using the deployment without having accepted.
- **Acceptance screen** (`/account/legal`, `auth/LegalAcceptanceGate`, inside `AuthLayout`): when an account hasn't accepted the newest *substantial* version of a document (a first publication, or a substantial change), `GET /auth/me` lists it in `legal_acceptance_pending` (`updated`: the account accepted an earlier version) and the frontend's status becomes `needs-legal-acceptance` (checked before the mandatory-TOTP gate; `RequireFullAccess` and `Home` redirect there). Titled "Updated terms" when any of them is a change to what the account accepted, "Before you continue" for a first publication. It lists the documents with their version, one checkbox, **Accept and continue**, **Sign out**, and a link to delete the account (Preferences → Privacy) for someone who doesn't accept.
- **Server-side**: while anything is pending, `get_current_user` answers 403 `LEGAL_ACCEPTANCE_REQUIRED` to every route except the account itself (`/auth/…`: who it is, sign out, sessions, security, deleting it), the documents (`/legal…`) and managing them (`/instance/legal…`, so publishing one document doesn't lock the operator out before the other is out).
- **About** links each published document (same dialog). A document missing in the user's language shows in the one it has, with a note.
- **Preferences → Privacy** (`account/AccountLegalPanel`, above deleting the account): the current version of each published document, to read, and every version the account accepted with its date (`GET /auth/legal/acceptances`). Hidden while nothing is published or accepted.

## Data
- `legal_document_draft` (`kind` PK, `content` JSONB language → text, `updated_at`/`updated_by_user_id`).
- `legal_document_version` (`kind` + `version` unique, `content`, `requires_acceptance`, `published_at`/`published_by_user_id`).
- `legal_acceptance` (`user_id`, `kind`, `version` unique together, `accepted_at` UTC): the proof, never updated; one line per acceptance in `auth.log` ("User … accepted terms v3, privacy v1 at signup"). No IP address or browser is stored with it.
- An account is up to date on a document when its highest accepted version ≥ the newest version with `requires_acceptance` (`legal_service.pending_acceptances`); signup accepts the latest version (even a minor one).

## Endpoints
- Public: `GET /legal` (latest version per published document: `kind`, `version`, `published_at`, `languages`), `GET /legal/{kind}?lang=` (404 `LEGAL_DOCUMENT_NOT_FOUND` when unpublished; `language`, `requested_language_available`, `content`).
- `GET /auth/legal/acceptances` (the caller's acceptances, newest first).
- `POST /auth/legal/accept` (CSRF; `acceptances` must name the current version of every pending document, else 400/409 as at signup).
- Instance administrator: `GET /instance/legal` (per document: draft, `has_unpublished_changes`, versions), `PUT /instance/legal/{kind}/draft` (unsupported languages dropped; 422 `LEGAL_TEXT_TOO_LONG` over 100 000 characters a language), `POST /instance/legal/{kind}/publish` (step-up; `requires_acceptance`; 422 `LEGAL_DRAFT_EMPTY`), `GET /instance/legal/{kind}/versions/{version}`, `POST /instance/legal/templates` (renders, saves nothing), `GET /instance/legal/acceptances.csv` (step-up). Publishing and exporting the acceptances are recorded in Platform's activity log (`docs/features/instance-tenancy.md#activity-log`); saving drafts and rendering templates aren't.
