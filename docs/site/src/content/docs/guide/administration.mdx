The **Admin** module is the Exercise Program Manager's workspace. Its sections are **Organization**, **Users and roles**, **Exercises**, **Allowed Domains**, **Library** and **Improvement plan**.

## Organization

The profile of the active organization, with its dashboard. **Edit profile** changes the name, alias, logo and tags. Deleting the organization removes every exercise in it, and can't be undone.

## Users and roles

A table of people against roles: Exercise Program Manager, Exercise Planning Team, Observer and Evaluator. A role here **authorizes** someone, and each exercise's team then assigns them.

<Screen name="users-and-roles" alt="Users and roles: people against the organization's roles" />

- **Add person** takes an email, roles and tags. On a deployment where you manage the instance's organizations, the field suggests accounts from your allowed domains that hold no role yet.
- On a deployment where anyone can create an organization, only people with an email in your organization's own domain (or already in the organization) are added at once. Anyone else gets an **invitation**: it waits in their account until they accept or decline it, and lapses after 14 days. You see it under **Pending invitations** and can withdraw it there. You never learn whether the address has an account until they accept.
- **Edit** changes roles and tags. You confirm by typing the person's name.
- **Remove** deletes their grants. Someone still on an exercise is disabled instead, until they're taken off it.

The organization always keeps one Exercise Program Manager.

## Allowed Domains

Only an email domain on this list can become an Exercise Planner or an Exercise Program Manager. An empty list allows nobody. Observers, Evaluators and Players aren't limited by it.

## Exercises

The organization's whole portfolio. You can archive or delete any of them. To work on one you need a seat on its team, so **Join** adds you with a role your grants allow.

## Founding another organization

Depending on [the instance's rule](/instance/tenancy/), the avatar menu offers **Create your own organization**.

## Leaving

**Preferences → Privacy** has **Leave** for the active organization. An Exercise Program Manager hands over first if they're the last one.
