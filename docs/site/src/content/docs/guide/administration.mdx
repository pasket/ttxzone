The **Admin** module is the Exercise Program Manager's workspace. Every list has its search at the top right and, under it, its filters, each with its name. Its sections are **Organization**, **Users and roles**, **Exercises**, **Allowed domains**, **Library** and **Improvement plan**.

## Organization

The profile of the active organization, with its dashboard. **Edit profile** changes the name, alias, logo and tags. Deleting the organization is at the end of that page, in a panel of its own: it removes every exercise in it, and can't be undone.

On the same page, **Context for exercise design** takes what your exercises are designed around, in your own words: the sector, the organization's size and footprint, the regulators and frameworks you answer to, your technologies and your critical assets and services. All of it is optional and saves apart from the profile. Where the [AI assistant](/guide/ai-assistant/) is on, your Exercise Planners' AI client reads it so its proposals fit your organization, which means it reaches their AI provider: write no personal data there.

Below it, **AI assistant** chooses whose AI client reaches your organization's exercises: Exercise Program Managers, Exercise Planners, Controllers and Facilitators, and Evaluators. Each person still connects a client of their own, and it reads only what their role already shows them. Program Managers, Planners and Evaluators start on; Controllers and Facilitators start off. Turning a role off stops its tokens on your organization without deleting them, and the change is recorded in the activity log. If your instance administrator has the assistant off, nobody uses it whatever you choose here.

## Users and roles

A table of people against roles: Exercise Program Manager, Exercise Planning Team, Observer and Evaluator. A role here **authorizes** someone, and each exercise's team then assigns them.

<Screen name="users-and-roles" alt="Users and roles: people against the organization's roles" />

- **Add person** takes a person, roles and tags. Where organizations aren't founded freely on the deployment, start typing a name or email: it suggests the accounts from your allowed domains that don't have a role here yet. Anyone else (an Observer or Evaluator from another domain) is added by typing their email. Where anyone can found an organization, it's an email field, and someone outside your organization's domain gets an invitation to accept.
- On a deployment where anyone can create an organization, only people with an email in your organization's own domain (or already in the organization) are added at once. Anyone else gets an **invitation**: it waits in their account until they accept or decline it, and lapses after 14 days. You see it under **Pending invitations** and can withdraw it there. You never learn whether the address has an account until they accept.
- **Edit** changes roles and tags. You confirm by typing the person's name.
- **Remove** deletes their grants. Someone still on an exercise is disabled instead, until they're taken off it.

The organization always keeps one Exercise Program Manager.

## Allowed domains

Only an email domain on this list can become an Exercise Planner or an Exercise Program Manager. An empty list allows nobody. Observers, Evaluators and Players aren't limited by it.

## Exercises

The organization's whole portfolio. You can archive or delete any of them; tick several to archive or unarchive them together. Without a seat on an exercise's team you can still read it: select its name (or **Read**) to open its profile, needs analysis, MSEL, session plan and after-action report, where nothing can be changed. You can also download each one's documents as PDFs (needs analysis, MSEL, session documents, after-action report) from **Documents** in its row, with or without a seat on its team. To work on one you need a seat on its team, so **Join** adds you with a role your grants allow.

## Founding another organization

Depending on [the instance's rule](/instance/tenancy/), the avatar menu offers **Create your own organization**.

## Leaving

**Preferences → Privacy** has **Leave** for the active organization. An Exercise Program Manager hands over first if they're the last one.
