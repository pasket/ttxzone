You run the exercise: you deliver the MSEL's injects and keep the Players in scope. You work in **Control**. Whoever creates an exercise plans and controls it; otherwise you need the Exercise Planner role in the organization, and the exercise's team makes you a Controller.

**Your tasks:**

- [Run the session from the Control center](/guide/conduct/control-center/)
- [Know what the table sees](/guide/conduct/playground/)

You can read the after-action report, and the session's exports and feedback results.
