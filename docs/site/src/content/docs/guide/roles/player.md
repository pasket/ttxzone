You take part in the exercise, working through a simulated incident with your team. Nothing touches real systems.

## Join

You'll get a link by email, which adds you at once, or a join code. Enter a code in **Playground → My exercises**, and an Exercise Planner approves your request. You can also ask to leave an exercise from there.

## Before the session

Read the Situation Manual if you've been given one. The Playground shows a summary and the session's details until it starts.

## During the session

- Read each inject as it arrives, and discuss it with your team.
- Keep private notes in your panel.
- Record what you decide, and vote when asked.
- **If a real incident happens**, press the stop keyword on the band at the top. [Anyone can](/guide/conduct/playground/#stopping-for-a-real-incident).

## Afterwards

Answer the [feedback form](/guide/evaluate/feedback/). Your answers are anonymous.
