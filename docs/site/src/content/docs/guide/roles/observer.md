You watch the session and take notes, without rating it. You act in the **Playground**, shown as an Observer. An Exercise Program Manager grants the role in the organization, and the exercise's team assigns you. If you're from outside the organization, you may get an invitation instead: accept it from the notice at the top of the page after you sign in.

**Your tasks:**

- [Find your way around the Playground](/guide/conduct/playground/)
- [Answer the feedback form](/guide/evaluate/feedback/)
