You moderate with the Players and keep the focus on the objectives. You act in the **Playground**, shown as a Facilitator. A Facilitator is also a Controller on the exercise, so the same organization role applies: Exercise Planner, then a seat on the exercise's team.

**In your panel** you have inject and general notes, the phase's key questions and the current inject's Facilitator notes, which the Players never see.

You can record decisions, put questions to a vote, close a vote and mark key questions covered.

**Your tasks:**

- [Find your way around the Playground](/guide/conduct/playground/)
- Before the session, read the Facilitator and Controller guide, among the [session documents](/guide/design/session-plan/#session-documents).
