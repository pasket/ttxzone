You design exercises in the **Design** module, with the rest of the planning and control team. One of you is the Lead Planner, who coordinates the team. An Exercise Program Manager adds you to the organization's Exercise Planning Team; then you create an exercise, or its team gives you a seat.

**Your tasks:**

- [Run your first exercise](/guide/first-exercise/)
- [Create the exercise and name the team](/guide/design/exercise-and-team/)
- [Complete the needs analysis](/guide/design/needs-analysis/)
- [Build the MSEL](/guide/design/msel/)
- [Prepare the session plan](/guide/design/session-plan/)
- [Bring in the Players](/guide/design/players/)
- [Write the after-action report](/guide/evaluate/after-action-report/)
