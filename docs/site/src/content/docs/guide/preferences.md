Open **Preferences** from the avatar menu.

## General

Language, avatar, and the light or dark theme. The theme is saved per device.

## Default workspace

The organization, exercise and role you land on after signing in.

## Security

- **Second factor.** Enroll an authenticator app, or use a code by email if the instance sends email. With both, you choose the preferred one. Some roles must use an authenticator app, and the page says so.
- **Backup codes.** Keep them safe: they get you in without your phone. Each works once.
- **Password.** Changing it ends your other sessions.
- **Active sessions.** See where you're signed in, and end sessions.
- **Single sign-on.** Link your account, if the instance uses it.

Changes to how you sign in (password, second factor, backup codes, AI assistant tokens, single sign-on) and deleting your account ask you to confirm it's you if you haven't done so in the last 15 minutes. Work in your organizations and exercises doesn't: approving, deleting with the name typed, joining or leaving goes ahead with the session you have, and the instance's activity log records who did it.

## Sessions

Sessions end after a period without activity, 30 minutes for the roles that need a second factor and 60 for the rest, and always 12 hours after signing in. You get a warning before the first of these.

Too many failed sign-ins lock the attempt for 15 minutes. Sessions already open aren't affected.

## Privacy

The versions of the legal documents you've accepted, leaving the active organization, and deleting your account.

## If you've lost your second factor

Use a backup code. Without one, ask an instance administrator to reset it.
