## Create an exercise

Select **Create exercise** in the top bar, or **New exercise** in the exercise list (**All exercises** in the top bar's exercise selector; select an exercise's name there to open it). Name it and choose its **content language**, the language its injects and material are written in. That's separate from anyone's interface language.

You become its **Lead Planner**, who coordinates the team, and you plan and control it. Each exercise gets a code such as `TTX-2026-01`.

You don't have to start from scratch: on **New exercise**, *Or start from an exercise template* lets you choose a [template](/guide/design/library/#exercise-templates) from the library (TTX-Zone's or your organization's) or import a `.ttx.md` file. It's checked first, you fill in its placeholders, and the new exercise is created from it.

A template's placeholders, such as `{brand}` or `{supplier}`, are the details that make the story yours. Each one says what it stands for, and **Where it appears** shows the sentences that use it. When the template suggests a value, select **Use** beside it, or **Use the suggested values** for all the ones you left blank. Nothing here is final: what you leave blank stays as `{placeholder}`, you can [fill them in together later](/guide/design/msel/#injects), and every text of the exercise can be edited once it's created.

## Name the team

Open **Exercise**. Under the figures, the team table shows everyone on the exercise except its Players, with what each one does, and its rooms when it's played in rooms. To change the team, select **Edit exercise**: the exercise's own details are saved with **Save changes**, while the team and the rooms apply as you change them.

**Add person** offers only people the organization has authorized. Tick what they do:

- **Design and conduct:** Exercise Planner, Controller, Facilitator.
- **Observation and evaluation:** Observer, Evaluator.

A Facilitator must also be a Controller. A person is an Evaluator or an Observer on an exercise, not both.

Each role needs its grant in the organization. A role without one is locked, and the dialog says which grant is missing. If you're the organization's Exercise Program Manager, a **Grant … in the organization** button gives it right there, when adding or editing someone (the same as Admin → Users and roles); then tick the role and save.

As an Exercise Program Manager, where organizations aren't founded freely on the deployment, **Add person** also lists **Not in the organization yet**: accounts from your allowed domains with no role. Adding one gives them the roles you tick in the organization too, in one step.

### Running an exercise on your own

One person can hold every team role on an exercise: Planner, Controller, Facilitator and Evaluator (or Observer). Switch between them with the **Role** selector in the top bar. To rehearse, run the session from Control and restart it with **delete** when you're done. To see what the table sees, use **View as Player** in the Playground; to vote as a Player, use a second account.

The flag beside a name makes that person the **Lead Planner** or the **Lead Evaluator**. The Lead Evaluator organizes the evaluation team and can write the report. Removing a lead asks you to name who takes over.

## Other actions

- **Duplicate** copies an exercise. The session plan isn't copied, since it's rebuilt from the copy's MSEL.
- **Archive** makes it read-only and takes it out of the default lists. You can undo it.
- **Delete** is permanent and asks for the exercise's name.

Duplicating needs you to plan the exercise. Archiving and deleting are for its Lead Planner or an Exercise Program Manager.
