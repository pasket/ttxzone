## Create an exercise

Select **Create exercise** in the top bar, or **New exercise** in the exercise list. Name it and choose its **content language**, the language its injects and material are written in. That's separate from anyone's interface language.

You become its **Lead Planner**, who coordinates the team, and you plan and control it. Each exercise gets a code such as `TTX-2026-01`.

You can also start from a [scenario](/guide/design/library/#scenarios).

## Name the team

Open **Exercise** and select **Edit exercise**. The team table shows everyone on the exercise except its Players, with what each one does.

**Add person** offers only people the organization has authorized. Tick what they do:

- **Design and conduct:** Exercise Planner, Controller, Facilitator.
- **Observation and evaluation:** Observer, Evaluator.

A Facilitator must also be a Controller. A person is an Evaluator or an Observer on an exercise, not both.

The flag beside a name makes that person the **Lead Planner** or the **Lead Evaluator**. The Lead Evaluator organizes the evaluation team and can write the report. Removing a lead asks you to name who takes over.

## Other actions

- **Duplicate** copies an exercise. The session plan isn't copied, since it's rebuilt from the copy's MSEL.
- **Archive** makes it read-only and takes it out of the default lists. You can undo it.
- **Delete** is permanent and asks for the exercise's name.

Duplicating needs you to plan the exercise. Archiving and deleting are for its Lead Planner or an Exercise Program Manager.
