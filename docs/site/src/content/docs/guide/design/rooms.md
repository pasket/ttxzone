An exercise can be played by several teams at once, each in its own room with its own injects. It's off by default.

## Set it up

In **Exercise**, select **Edit exercise**, then turn on rooms in the panel below the team. Add the rooms and seat each Player. A Player without a room is in the main room only.

## How it works

- **A Player sees only where they are.** The Controller decides that, and can bring everyone to the **main room**.
- **An inject is played in all rooms by default**, or in the ones you choose.
- **The main room has no injects of its own.** The Controller can project an already delivered inject there.
- **The team picks any room to look at**, and sees exactly what its Players see. Each team member can have a default room.
- **Notes, EEG ratings and key questions** are per room.

## What gets locked

Once the MSEL is ready or a session exists, you can't turn rooms on or off, or add or remove rooms. Renaming a room and seating Players stay open, even during the session. A room that injects are aimed at can't be removed.
