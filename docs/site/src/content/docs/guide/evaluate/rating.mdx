Evaluators work in the **Playground**, in their panel, during the session.

## Rate a point

By default the panel shows your assigned EEG points for the current inject. A toggle shows all of yours, and a counter shows how many you've rated.

For each point, choose a level and give your reason:

| Level | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **P** | Performed without challenges: done as expected and on time, following plans and procedures |
| **S** | Performed with some challenges |
| **M** | Performed with major challenges |
| **U** | Unable to be performed: not done, or done in a way that doesn't achieve what was expected |

A point may have its own definition of each level, and the picker shows it.

## Notes

Add an **Inject note** or **General notes** as you go. They reach the report's authors afterwards.

## Evaluator or Observer

An Observer only watches and takes notes. An Evaluator rates. If you're on the planning and control team and also evaluate, pick **Evaluator** in the role selector.
