Once the session is over, Players, Facilitators, Observers and Evaluators find the **Feedback form** in their panel. It has statements rated from 1 to 5, one for each objective, and a few open questions. Every answer is optional.

Answers are anonymous by construction: TTX-Zone records who answered, so nobody answers twice, separately from what they said. The two are never linked.

Controllers, Exercise Planners and the Lead Evaluator see the results in the session summary, once there are at least three answers. The report's authors see them while writing.

A paper version is among the [session documents](/guide/design/session-plan/#session-documents).
