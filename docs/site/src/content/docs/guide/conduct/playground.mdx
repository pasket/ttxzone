The **Playground** is the live session. Everyone sees the same thing, so an Evaluator rates exactly what the table saw. Only the panel on the right changes with your role.

<Screen name="playground" alt="A Player's Playground during a session" />

## What's on screen

- A band reading **EXERCISE · EXERCISE · EXERCISE**, always visible.
- The phases, with the scenario's date and time. Future phases have no name, so nobody sees what's ahead.
- The inject **In play**, with its images, files and **Questions for the table**.
- Who's present.
- The decisions recorded so far.

New injects appear on their own and never take focus from what you're typing. If you reload or join late, you see the same state as everyone else.

## Your panel

| Role | Panel |
|---|---|
| Player | The table's decisions, and private notes grouped by inject |
| Facilitator | Inject and general notes, the key questions and the inject's Facilitator notes |
| Observer | Inject and general notes |
| Evaluator | The EEG points of the current inject, ratings and notes |

## Files

Files arrive with their inject. Each shows its size, description and SHA-256, so the table can check what it's analysing. Text files can be previewed.

## Decisions and votes

Players, Facilitators and Controllers record what the table decided. A vote shows under the stage: pick an option, and the tally updates. Votes are anonymous. The Controller shows the result when it's time, and impact meters if the exercise has them.

<Screen name="vote" alt="A Player voting on a poll during a session" />

## Stopping for a real incident

The band always shows the stop keyword. **Anyone can use it, Players included.**

While the session runs, the keyword is a button. Press it, type the keyword to confirm, and the session pauses for everyone. Every screen turns red and shows who paused it and when. Only a Controller can resume it.

There's no password prompt, so nothing delays it.

## After the session

A closing screen appears, and the **Feedback form** is in your panel.
